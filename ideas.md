## Selected Approach: Neo-Brutalism / High-Contrast Utility
I will proceed with the **Neo-Brutalism / High-Contrast Utility** style. This fits the "tool" nature of the job scraper perfectly. It emphasizes raw data, speed, and efficiency, which aligns with the user's goal of processing hundreds of jobs. The high contrast will make scanning job lists easy, and the bold aesthetic will make the app feel powerful and robust.

**Design Philosophy**: "The Power Tool for Job Hunting"
- **Colors**: Black (#000000), White (#FFFFFF), Electric Blue (#2563EB) for accents, Warning Yellow (#FACC15) for alerts.
- **Typography**: JetBrains Mono for headers/data, Inter for body text.
- **Shapes**: Sharp corners, thick borders.
- **Vibe**: Professional, efficient, slightly technical.
