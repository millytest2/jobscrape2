## How to actually run this — the part that decides the outcome

Volume applications to job boards convert at roughly 2–3%. The plan below is built to beat that, and it is deliberately small enough that you'll actually do it.

**Weekly targets: 10 applications, 15 recruiter/hiring-manager messages, 5 warm referral asks.**

**The rule that matters most: never apply cold.** For every application, find the hiring manager or a recruiter on LinkedIn and send a short note the same day. An application plus a message converts several times better than an application alone. `outreach/` has the templates.

**Do this in order for each role:**
1. Pick the matching variant and open the JD.
2. Paste the JD into the keyword checker (`python3 keyword_check.py <variant> <jd.txt>`). Add any genuinely-true missing keyword to your skills line. Do not add anything you cannot discuss for two minutes in an interview.
3. Rename the file `Miles_Tipton_<Company>_<Role>.docx`. Recruiters see the filename.
4. Submit `.docx` unless the posting explicitly asks for PDF — Workday and iCIMS parse Word more reliably.
5. Same day: message the hiring manager and one recruiter.
6. Log it in `application_tracker.csv`.
7. Follow up on day 4 and day 10 if no reply. Most people never follow up; this is free advantage.

**Sequencing note:** don't fire at your top 5 dream companies in week one. Apply to 5–10 Tier 2 and Tier 4 roles first, do those screens, and get your $60K story and your "why so many roles" answer sharp under real pressure. Then go at Tier 1 and Tier 3. You only get one shot per company per role, so spend the early reps where a miss costs you least.
